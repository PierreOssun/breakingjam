# Name review

Matches the harvest left for Pierre to decide; nothing here is merged in `data/`.

- **`fleg` and `dj-fleg`** (both US, both DJs): `fleg` in Outbreak Europe 2014–2016, `dj-fleg` in Red Bull BC One 2018 and the Olympics. A UMD profile says Fleg DJed BC One and Outbreak, so probably one person.
- **`plash` and `dj-plash-one`** (both PL, both DJs): `plash` in Outbreak Europe 2014–2016, `dj-plash-one` in Red Bull BC One 2021 and the Olympics. His SoundCloud lists BC One 2021, so probably one person.
- **Merged:** `pacpac` into `pac-pac` (BOTY 2019 titles spell him both ways; `pacpac` was only in that draft) and `floorriorz` into `flooriorz` (BOTY's own spelling; the duplicate was only in the Freestyle Session 2017 draft).
- **"Flying Step" in a BOTY 2021 title** is taken as Flying Steps (DE).
- **Reused for BOTY 2019's b-girls:** `fanny` (FR), `firebird` (BG), `karina` (EE) and `eri-fenesis` (JP), from earlier jams; same people or homonyms?
- **Outbreak Europe kids, reused keys:** the 2018 kids champions "Raptor & Malik" use `raptor` (UA, the 2026 1v1 champion) and `malik` (FR); "Grom" (2017, 2019, flagged RU on Wikipedia) uses `grom` (ZZ, Formless Corp). Same people?
- **Removed typo keys** `phil-wizzard` and `phill-wizard` (Outbreak 2018 draft only); both are `phil-wizard`.
- **Battle Pro spellings folded into one key:** Arabiq Flavor → `arabiq-flavour`, EastSide Bboys → `east-side-bboys`, Future Crew → `the-future-crew`, Predatoz → `predatorz`, Jinjo → `jinjo-crew`, Momentum → `momentum-crew` (an unused `momentum` crew is still in `crews.toml`), Red Bull BC One All-Star → `red-bull-bc-one-all-stars`.
- **Battle Pro drafts, homonyms?** `omar` (US) is the German Omar of 2014; `jazzy` (TR) is the Dutch Jazzy, or "Jessy", of 2015.
- **`yu-seng`** stays ZZ: Wikipedia flags him Swiss at Battle Pro 2016 and Chinese at the UK Championships 2012; he is also in Battle of Sun 2026.
- **UK Championships:** Amaro (DZ, 2014) got his own key `amaro-dz`, apart from the Italian `amaro`; Russia's Dream Team (2016) got `dream-team-ru`, apart from Battle Pro's German `dream-team`; Team Shmetta is `shmetta-skwad`, Smack 19 is `smac-19`, Gun Smoke Breakers is `gunsmoke-breakers`, Chen Chen is `chenchen`.
- **Fixed `stefani`:** GB → UA (Wikipedia flags her UKR at BC One, Outbreak and the Olympics; GB was a draft misparse).
- **IBE, check:** "Katsu" of 2015's Japan Seven 2 Smoke team is keyed `katsu`, not `katsu1`; 2012 Seven 2 Smoke winner Blond is flagged KOR there, while `blond` is AU; 2024's crew finalist "Formless" uses the `formless` crew, not `formless-corp`; same ones?
- **WDSF Worlds 2023, countries:** WDSF lists Mighty Jimm under Belgium (`mighty-jimm` is GR) and Alvin under Colombia (`alvin` is VE); the flag a dancer competes under or the one they are from? Left as they were.
- **WDSF Worlds 2023, new judge keys:** `kim` (Pei Dongguang, CN), `vee` (Elvina Da Silva, ZA), `steez` (Yuta Tsugumo, JP), `manny` (Emmanuel Adelekun, GB), `lazylegz` (Luca Patuelli, CA); `kim` and `vee` are short enough to collide with later dancers.
- **`max` and `malik`:** `max` is an MC of Outbreak Europe 2018 and `malik` a 2018 kids champion. The Olympics' hosts Max (PT) and Malik (FR, an adult MC) were left out as unconfirmed; if added later, Malik needs a key of his own.
