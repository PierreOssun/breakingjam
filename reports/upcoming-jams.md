# Upcoming jams (found 15 Nov 2026)

Breaking jams from 16 November 2026 onward whose date a source states, gathered so they can be entered on the site. `data/` can't hold them: `data check` accepts only jams that are over, and jams still to come are entered on the site (as the team, so their page ends with "You're the organiser? Contact us"). Production had 197 jams and none upcoming on 15 Nov 2026.

- **109 jams** in 29 countries, 19 Nov 2026 to 30 Oct 2027: 69 in Nov–Dec 2026. 69 are *high* confidence (the organiser, a federation or a ticket page states the date and city), 40 *medium* (a third-party listing or news article only).
- **Every field the site needs** is in `data/upcoming.toml`, as the API takes them: dates, timezone, city, country, venue, address, pin, battles (with bracket size, prize and start time when announced), website, Instagram, registration and source links. `just seed` (and so `just reset-db`) enters the ones not over yet.
- **Not publishable yet:** the publish checklist wants an Instagram link or a contact email, and at least one battle.
  - No Instagram or email (6): City Battle (Écourt-Saint-Quentin), Flow Elements (Cagliari), and the four Mynavi DANCEALIVE qualifiers (contact form only).
  - Battles not announced (16): their date is confirmed but not their categories yet (e.g. Outbreak Europe 2027, UK B-Boy Championships 2027, Find your Flow 2027).
- **How it was searched:** five searches by region (international circuits; France, Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg; the rest of Europe; the Americas; Asia-Pacific, Middle East and Africa) on and8.dance, federation calendars, organisers' sites, ticketing pages, local agendas, kepasa.fr (French hip hop events) and [breaking-calendar.com](https://breaking-calendar.com/en), a worldwide breaking calendar whose home page carries its whole event list. Pins come from Photon (OpenStreetMap); "city coords" in the notes means the venue isn't known yet.
- **Thin coverage:** the session's web-search budget ran out partway through, so these are under-searched: the USA and Canada's local jams, Latin America outside Brazil, Spain, Portugal, Ireland, Scandinavia, Central and Eastern Europe (Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Ukraine…), Southeast Asia, India, Oceania and Africa, and France's spring 2027 season (the federation's site blocks scripts).
